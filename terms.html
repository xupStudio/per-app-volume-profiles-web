3. iOS Volume Model & Background Playback Limits

iOS and iPadOS provide one global media volume, not a separate audio mixer for every app. When an app enters the foreground, the App uses Shortcuts to adjust that global media volume to the value in the active Mode.

If an audio app keeps playing in the background, a rule for the next foreground app still changes the same global media volume. The App cannot independently control two simultaneous audio streams, and it cannot guarantee that Apple Shortcuts will always execute instantly, without interruption, or without system delay.

4. Bluetooth Headphone Volume Limits

Separate headphone volume supports only Bluetooth headphones selected in an Apple Shortcuts Bluetooth Personal Automation. You must install the Connect and Disconnect helper shortcuts and create separate connect and disconnect automations.

Wired headphones are not supported. Apple does not provide a corresponding wired-headphone automation trigger, and iOS does not reliably expose the current audio output to the App. The App records the state reported by the helper automations.
