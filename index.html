FAQ

常見問題

Frequently Asked Questions

iOS 真的能做 per-app 音量？ Can iOS really do per-app volume?

可以，而且完全合法。App 用 iOS 內建的自動化機制，幫你在開啟特定 App 時自動套用對應音量。不需要越獄，也不會碰到系統的東西。

Yes, and completely legitimately. The app uses iOS's built-in automation to apply the matching volume when you open a specific app. No jailbreak, no system tampering.

支援 Apple Watch / Mac 嗎？ Does it work on Apple Watch or Mac?

目前只做 iPhone 與 iPad。Apple Watch / Mac 不在這次的範圍內。

iPhone and iPad only for now. Apple Watch and Mac aren't in scope.

價格怎麼算？ How much does it cost?

免費版可建立 1 個自訂設定檔。Pro 解鎖無限設定檔、進階觸發、聽力健康、iCloud 分享。

Free gets 1 custom profile. Pro unlocks unlimited profiles, advanced triggers, Hearing Health, and iCloud sharing.

會耗電嗎？ Will it drain my battery?

不會。App 不在背景跑，只在你打開來編輯設定檔時運作。

No. The app doesn't run in the background — only when you open it to edit profiles.
