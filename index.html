FEATURES

為 iOS 打造的音量管理

Volume management built for iOS

用 Apple 官方的「捷徑」路徑，讓常打開的 App 回到你指定的音量。

Use Apple's official Shortcuts path to return the apps you open to the volume you chose.

每個 App 的音量規則

A Volume Rule for Each App

在情境模式中加入 App，設定 0–100% 的媒體音量。App 打開時，自動化會套用目前模式的規則。

Add apps to a Mode and choose a 0–100% media volume. When an app opens, the automation applies the active Mode's rule.

情境模式

Modes

為夜間、公共場所、遊戲或社群 App 準備不同組合。切換模式，不必重建自動化。

Choose a headphone volume per app. Two helper automations switch output state when selected Bluetooth headphones connect and disconnect.

FAQ

常見問題

Frequently Asked Questions

iOS 真的能為不同 App 設定音量嗎？Can iOS really set a different volume for each app?

App Volume 不會建立多個音訊混音器。它使用 Apple「捷徑」的 App 開啟觸發與「設定音量」動作，在某個 App 進入前景時調整 iOS 唯一的媒體音量。

App Volume does not create separate audio mixers. It uses Apple's Shortcuts App trigger and Set Volume action to adjust iOS's single media volume when an app enters the foreground.

每個 App 都要建立一條自動化嗎？Do I need a separate automation for every app?

不用。iOS / iPadOS 18.2 以上只要一條 App 個人自動化，在觸發條件中一次勾選所有要控制的 App。之後新增 App 規則時，記得把該 App 加進同一個觸發選擇器。

No. On iOS / iPadOS 18.2 or later, one App Personal Automation can contain every app you control. When you add a rule later, remember to add that app to the same trigger picker.

耳機音量支援哪些耳機？Which headphones work with separate headphone volume?

支援你能在「捷徑」藍牙個人自動化中選擇的藍牙耳機。需要連線與中斷兩個 helper 自動化。有線耳機不支援，因為這套流程依賴 Apple 的藍牙連線觸發。

It works with Bluetooth headphones you can select in a Shortcuts Bluetooth Personal Automation. Two helper automations cover connect and disconnect. Wired headphones are unsupported because the setup relies on Apple's Bluetooth connection trigger.

背景播放有什麼限制？What are the limits with background audio?

iOS 只有一個全域媒體音量，且自動化在 App 進入前景時觸發。音樂留在背景播放後，如果你再打開另一個有規則的 App，該規則會改變同一個系統音量；App Volume 無法替兩個同時播放的 App 維持不同音量。

iOS has one global media volume, and the automation runs when an app enters the foreground. If music keeps playing in the background and you open another app with a rule, that rule changes the same system volume. App Volume cannot maintain two simultaneous per-stream volumes.

App Volume 要付費或訂閱嗎？Does App Volume require payment or a subscription?

目前 App Store 上的版本完全免費，沒有訂閱。

The current App Store version is free to use and has no subscription.

支援 Apple Watch、Mac 或 CarPlay 嗎？Does it support Apple Watch, Mac, or CarPlay?

目前只支援 iPhone 與 iPad，不提供 Apple Watch、Mac 或 CarPlay 專用功能。

The current version supports iPhone and iPad only, with no dedicated Apple Watch, Mac, or CarPlay features.
