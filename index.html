FAQ

常見問題

Frequently Asked Questions

這是怎麼做到 per-app 音量的？iOS 不是不允許嗎？ How does this achieve per-app volume? iOS doesn't allow it, right?

iOS 確實沒開放系統音量 API，所以我們不去 hack。我們合法地組合 iOS 既有的 Shortcuts「Set Volume」action 與 Personal Automation「Open App」trigger，App 內幫你產生對應的 Shortcuts 與設定指南。設定完之後，每次打開那個 App，iOS 會自動執行對應 Shortcut 來調整音量。

iOS doesn't expose a system volume API, so we don't hack it. We legitimately combine iOS's existing Shortcuts "Set Volume" action with the "When App Is Opened" Personal Automation trigger. The app generates the Shortcuts and walks you through setup. After that, opening the app triggers iOS to run the matching Shortcut.

需要每個 App 都設定一次嗎？很麻煩耶。 Do I have to set this up for every single app? That sounds tedious.

iOS 18.2 之後不用了。Apple 在 18.2 開放 「Get Current App」 Shortcuts action，讓我們可以用一條 Automation 管所有 App — 整套設定只跑一次。iOS 18.1 以下的版本仍需逐 App 設定，App 內精靈幫你一條一條建立、不會漏。

On iOS 18.2+, no. Apple added the "Get Current App" Shortcuts action in 18.2, letting us use one automation to cover every app — setup runs once. On iOS 18.1 or earlier, per-app setup is required, but our wizard guides you through each one.

支援 Apple Watch / Mac 嗎？ Does it work on Apple Watch or Mac?

v1.0.0 不支援。專注做好 iPhone 與 iPad（含 iPad 的 Stage Manager / Split View 多 App 場景）。Apple Watch / Mac 未來版本評估，但目前不在 roadmap。

Not in v1.0.0. We're focused on iPhone and iPad (including iPad's Stage Manager / Split View multi-app scenarios). Apple Watch and Mac are not on the current roadmap.

價格怎麼算？有免費版嗎？ What's the pricing? Is there a free tier?

免費版：1 個設定檔、3 條規則，5 組內建情境可預覽。Pro 解鎖無限設定檔、無限規則、進階觸發（Focus Mode、時段、耳機分流）、聽力健康整合、iCloud 分享。

Free: 1 profile, 3 rules, preview of 5 built-in templates. Pro unlocks unlimited profiles, unlimited rules, advanced triggers (Focus Mode, time of day, headphone routing), Hearing Health integration, and iCloud sharing.

會不會耗電？背景一直跑嗎？ Will it drain my battery? Does it run in the background?

不會背景跑。實際執行音量切換的是 iOS Shortcuts 系統，我們的 App 只在你打開來建立 / 編輯設定檔時運作。HealthKit 也用 background delivery，不主動 polling。

It doesn't. iOS Shortcuts itself handles the volume change — our app only runs when you open it to create or edit profiles. HealthKit uses background delivery, not active polling.

為什麼 icon 寫 AppVolume、App 內又叫 Per-App Volume Profiles？ Why is the icon labeled "AppVolume" but the app is called "Per-App Volume Profiles"?

Per-App Volume Profiles 是完整正式名（在 App Store、設定頁、行銷文案使用）。AppVolume 是主畫面 icon 短名 — iOS 主畫面 icon 標籤空間只夠 ~11 字，所以用簡短版本。兩者代表同一個 App。

Per-App Volume Profiles is the full official name (used in App Store, settings, marketing). AppVolume is the Home Screen icon shorthand — iOS only fits ~11 characters under an icon, so we use the short form. Same app.
